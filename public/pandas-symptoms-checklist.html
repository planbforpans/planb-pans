How doctors read this

The published PANS criteria look for a sudden onset of OCD or severely restricted eating, plus at least two of the other groups above, not better explained by another condition. PANDAS adds a link to strep and often includes tics. Read more: the full symptoms guide and how to get a diagnosis.
